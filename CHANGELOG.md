# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release:
- **Shard limit**: choose the most soul shards to carry (10 by default)
- **Hotkey**: each press deletes one shard over your limit. Set it in the game's Key Bindings, under Soul Warden
- **Macro**: `/soulwarden delete` does the same from a macro; the window can create the macro for you
- Shards are only deleted from your normal bags, **never from a soul bag**
- Optional chat message for each deleted shard
- Minimap button that opens the window, with a badge showing how many shards you're over your limit and your shard count in its tooltip
